**2025–27 cycle rules** ([iplt20](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/ipl-governing-council-announces-tata-ipl-player-regulations-2025-27), [Wisden](https://www.wisden.com/series/ipl-2025/cricket-news/explained-the-new-ipl-player-regulations-and-their-implications-indian-premier-league-2025), [Outlook](https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/cricket/indian-premier-league-2026-auction-overseas-player-salary-cap-explained-18-crore)):
- An overseas player who skipped the mega auction can't enter the minis.
- **Overseas pay cap in minis is ₹18cr**; the excess goes to the BCCI (Indian players exempt). Whether the purse is charged the full hammer price is **UNVERIFIED**.
- A player who is bought and then withdraws gets a 2-season ban.
- Uncapped rule: no international starting XI and no central contract in the last 5 years.
- **Impact Player** continues through 2027; with an overseas Impact Player, at most 3 overseas in the XI.

**Base prices and increments (2025 cycle):**
- Uncapped base: ₹30 / 40 / 50L.
- Capped base: ₹75L, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2cr.
- Increments: +5L up to 1cr; +10L for 1–2cr; +20L for 2–5cr; +25L above 5cr.
- Earlier years' increments: UNVERIFIED.

**Trades before 2027** ([Crictracker](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-news/ipl-2027-full-list-of-confirmed-trades-ahead-of-the-auction/)):
- Pant to DC (₹15cr).
- Kuldeep to LSG (₹13.5cr).
- CSK–SRH four-player swap.
- Hardik to PBKS for Arshdeep: reported, not official.

## 4. Known data traps (also listed in the register, section G)
- **Currency:** USD for 2008–13, INR from 2014. Some Kaggle sets convert silently (2013 USD × 75). Keep 1cr = 100L.
- **Hammer price ≠ pay:** the 2010/2012 tie-break excess went to the BCCI (Pollard recorded at $750k); 2026 Green hammered at 25.20cr but paid 18cr.
- **Retention cost ≠ salary** (2011, 2014, 2018).
- **Trades revise fees** (Jadeja 18 → 14cr). Trade windows open before the retention deadline and after the auction.
- **Accelerated rounds:** one player can appear twice. Separate first-pass lots from re-entries.
- **Replacements** (often undisclosed fees). **Players bought then withdrawn** still show as sold.
- **Bought but never played:** Cricsheet only has players who played. Join on Register IDs.
- **Mini-auction purse:** carried-over balance + top-up + released salaries. 2026 sources disagree on balances (e.g. KKR 64.3 vs 63.4cr).
