# Launch playbook (LinkedIn + Instagram)

*Started 12 Nov 2026. Living notes for the public launch — refine as the build lands. **Nothing is posted until it's built and you (Harsh) post it yourself** (rule #5: ask before anything public; you own the LinkedIn voice). This file is ideas + guardrails, not final copy.*

## Goal (be honest with ourselves)
Primary: **a credible, novel portfolio piece** that shows off deep agent-building craft and starts conversations. Virality is a *bonus, not a plan* — true viral from ~1k followers is low single-digit percent; "punches above 1k" (reshares, a few k views, real new followers, DMs) is achievable with good packaging + timing. Plan for it to NOT go viral; the win stands either way.

## The real levers (follower count barely matters — reshares do)
1. **A visual demo clip** (15–30s): agents *thinking and bidding* — the Scout/Treasurer/Strategist war-room arguing, the board + purse bars updating, a bidding war resolving. This is the single highest-leverage asset.
2. **A 3-second hook** (first line / first frame).
3. **Seed it to 3–5 resharers** in AI or cricket-analytics who might amplify. One reshare > your whole follower list.
4. **Two-post structure** with a payoff (below).

## Timing — the auction is the wave; ride it
| When | Post | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **~15 Dec** (day before auction) | **Tease:** "I built 10 AI agents, one per IPL team, and locked their 2027 auction predictions — here's the hash proving I can't edit them after." | IPL-auction audience is already warming up; the hash is the credibility hook |
| **16 Dec** | (watch; maybe a live/story thread as lots resolve) | real-time engagement if you have the live companion |
| **~17 Dec** | **Reveal:** the 5-way scorecard — AI agents vs pundits vs rules vs reality, calibration plot. | the payoff; "called it, here's the receipt" (or "here's what it got wrong") |

## Hook drafts (refine later with real content)
LinkedIn (technical/AI framing):
- *"I gave 10 open-source AI agents one IPL team each, a memory, a war-room, and a purse — then locked their auction predictions before the real auction. Hash attached. Here's how they reason."*

Instagram (visual, IPL-hashtag framing — a Reel):
- *"10 AI 'franchises' just drafted their IPL 2027 squads before the real auction. Watch them bid 🧵"* + the demo clip.

Platform split:
- **LinkedIn** = the engineering story (the agent architecture, memory/reflection/sub-agents, the pre-registration method). Audience: AI/tech.
- **Instagram** = the *watchable* clip + IPL emotion + a clean one-liner. Reels have the breakout path.

## Caption guardrails (these protect you — overclaiming gets the bad kind of viral)
- Say **"I couldn't find anyone who's done this,"** NOT "first" (rule #7).
- **No betting / odds framing** (rule #6).
- **No IPL / BCCI / team logos, no broadcast footage, no real auctioneer's name/voice/face** — your own visuals + generic persona only.
- State the honest caveat where relevant: the Dec-2025 backtest is contamination-prone; **the 16 Dec live run is the clean test** — which is exactly *why* the pre-commit hash matters. Lean into that, don't hide it.
- If predictions flop, **post the scorecard anyway** — "what my AI got wrong and why" is honest and often more relatable than a victory lap.
