Most common data-vs-Wikipedia disagreements (data first):

- allrounder vs bowler: 73
- batter vs allrounder: 67

- batter vs wicketkeeper: 38
- allrounder vs batter: 24

Most are borderline cases (a batter who bowls a few overs; a bowler Wikipedia calls an
all-rounder). Data wins when it has 10+ matches, because it shows what the player did in
the last three years, not his career label.
